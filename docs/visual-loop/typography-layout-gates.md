# Marketplace Typography And Layout Gates

Use these gates on the complete generated master after product identity and facts have been checked.

## Layer Freeze

- Treat the complete generated design as the final candidate.
- Freeze every passed complete master by hash. Retry only the failed region in a new version.

## 1000 px Square Hierarchy

- Safe margin: at least 48 px.
- Dominant numeric claim: 110-180 px when it is the primary message.
- Main headline: 56-84 px, one or two lines.
- Use one visual anchor: product, screen, measurement path, or one dominant number.

## Commerce Readability

Render each frame at about 300 x 300 px. Pass only when:
1. Product category or main benefit is readable without zooming.
2. One dominant claim wins immediately.
3. Product remains recognizable and is not hidden by text.
4. Supporting facts do not compete with the headline.

## Product Integrity

- Preserve approved product natural proportions and visible structure.
- Do not use perspective or 3D transforms that flatten a front cutout.
- Full-product promise requires the entire body, top geometry, buttons, ports, and visible thickness.

## Measurement Physics

- When top apertures are visible, start the beam at the verified small emitter aperture.
- When a low forward view hides the apertures, align the beam with the device axis from the far measuring end.
- End it on a visible opaque target.
